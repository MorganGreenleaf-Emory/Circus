Where Infectious Disease Meets Substance Use

The Infectious Disease Circus is an interdisciplinary collaborative advancing research, clinical practice, and public health at the intersection of infectious diseases and substance use disorders. We bring together clinicians, researchers, and advocates to address one of the most pressing co-occurring epidemics of our time.

Recent Research

HIV / Substance Use

Integrated Care Models Reduce HIV Viral Load in PWUD

J. Alvarez, S. Park, M. Okonkwo  ·  2025

A retrospective cohort study of 640 patients found that co-located addiction medicine and HIV care services were associated with a 34% increase in viral suppression rates compared to siloed care delivery.

Hepatitis C / MOUD

Buprenorphine Co-Administration Does Not Impair HCV DAA Efficacy

L. Chen, R. Vasquez, T. Nwosu  ·  2025

A prospective analysis of 210 patients on medications for opioid use disorder (MOUD) receiving direct-acting antiviral therapy demonstrates comparable sustained virologic response rates to the general population.

Read more →
Endocarditis / IVDU

Outpatient Parenteral Antibiotic Therapy Outcomes in IVDU-Associated Endocarditis

A. Reyes, D. Kim, P. Osei  ·  2026

This multicenter observational study evaluates the safety and effectiveness of OPAT programs for infective endocarditis in people who inject drugs, identifying predictors of successful completion.

ID / Addiction Medicine

Put us in, Coach: The Case for Recognizing the ID/Addiction Workforce

A. W. Fujita, E. F. Eaton, A. N. Nyaku, A. J. Schranz et al.  ·  2026

An advocacy paper in Clinical Infectious Diseases arguing for formal recognition of infectious disease physicians who specialize in addiction medicine, highlighting their unique role in treating patients with injection-related infections and opioid use disorder.

Injection-Related Infections / Oral Antibiotics

Low Use of Oral Antibiotics for People Hospitalized with Serious Injection-Related Infections: the CHOICE+ Cohort

A. W. Fujita et al.  ·  2025

A cohort study in Open Forum Infectious Diseases examining real-world uptake of oral antibiotic step-down therapy among hospitalized people who inject drugs, revealing persistent underutilization despite growing evidence of efficacy in serious infections.
